Frequently Asked Questions

Get quick answers to common queries

What are the class timings at Selection Darpan Hisar?
We offer multiple morning, afternoon, and evening batches starting from 9:00 AM to 6:30 PM to suit both regular students and working professionals. Contact our front desk for specific batch availability.
Are demo classes available before taking admission?
Absolutely! We offer 2 days of free trial demo classes for new students to experience our teaching methodology, shortcut tricks, and environment firsthand before paying any fees.
Do you provide study materials and mock test series?
Yes, complete printed assignment books, daily practice sheets (DPPs), updated Haryana G.K. notes, and weekly CBT / OMR mock test series are completely included in the main course fee.
Is there any hostel facility available nearby?
While we don't have our own hostel, the institute is located in Red Square Market where dozens of safe, highly-rated PG and hostel accommodations are available for both boys and girls within a 500-meter walking distance.
How are student doubt queries handled?
We run dedicated daily Doubt Desks. Faculties are personally available after regular lectures to sit with students and resolve individual queries step-by-step. We ensure no student goes home with an unsolved doubt.
