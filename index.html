Our Premium Courses

Syllabus structured for success

SSC CGL (Graduate Level)

  • Complete coverage of Tier-1 & Tier-2 syllabus.
  • Daily 3 hours of Concept + Solution classes.
  • Special high-level content from Delhi & Mukherjee Nagar.
  • Topic-wise weekly test series and analysis.
  • Advanced Mathematics short-tricks sessions.
  • English Comprehension & Vocab building daily.
  • Reasoning logic and time-management skills.
  • Daily General Awareness & Current Affairs updates.
  • One-to-One mentorship for doubt solving.
  • Daily targets provided to ensure self-study.

SSC CHSL & MTS

  • Targeted batches for 10+2 and 10th base exams.
  • Basic to Advanced level Mathematics foundation.
  • Special focus on English Grammar rules.
  • Static GK and History/Polity summary notes.
  • Previous Year Questions (PYQ) daily practice.
  • Typing test guidance and strategy sessions.
  • Calculation speed booster classes.
  • Printed assignment booklets for all subjects.
  • Monthly Mega-Tests to track overall rank.
  • Personalized strategy for non-maths students.

Delhi Police & SSC CPO

  • Dedicated syllabus for Central Police Forces.
  • Special Computer Knowledge classes for DP.
  • Physical Efficiency Test (PET) written strategy.
  • English Mains (200 Marks) special batch for CPO.
  • Indian Constitution & Law basic coverage.
  • Current Affairs specifically tailored for police exams.
  • Geography and Science quick-revision modules.
  • Strict daily attendance and progress tracking.
  • Offline OMR-based test practice environment.
  • Motivation and guidance from selected sub-inspectors.

HSSC & Haryana Police

  • Expert classes for Haryana Police Constable & SI.
  • In-depth Haryana G.K. by Dr. Vinod Sir.
  • Haryana Current Affairs and State Schemes covered.
  • Agriculture & Animal Husbandry special notes.
  • Maths & Reasoning according to HSSC pattern.

  • Haryana CET Pre & Mains targeted syllabus.
  • Group-C & Group-D separate practice sets.
  • State Administration (Gram Sachiv, Patwari) focus.
  • Doubt counters available till evening.
