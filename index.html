Frequently Asked Questions

Clear your doubts before you join

Do you provide Library/Hostel facilities?
Yes, we have an in-house 24/7 fully AC Digital Library equipped with high-speed Wi-Fi and individual charging points. While we do not have our own hostel, we have tie-ups with several safe, hygienic, and affordable PG/hostels within a 500-meter radius of the institute for both boys and girls.
What is the duration of the SSC CGL Foundation batch?
The comprehensive SSC CGL (Tier 1 + 2) Foundation batch typically takes 7 to 8 months to complete. This includes thorough syllabus coverage, daily practice sessions, and weekly full-length mock tests. We also offer crash courses (2-3 months) closer to exam dates.
Can I attend a demo class before paying the fee?
Absolutely! We encourage transparency. Every prospective student is entitled to 2 to 3 days of completely free Demo Classes. You can sit with regular batches, interact with teachers, evaluate the study material, and then make your admission decision.
Is the study material included in the fee?
Yes, the course fee is inclusive of the complete "Darpan Publication" study kit. This includes 15+ printed module books, daily class worksheets, current affairs magazines, and a free subscription to our Online Mock Test application valid for 1 year.
What if I miss a class due to illness?
No worries. We have a hybrid system in place. Important lectures are recorded, and backup classes can be arranged. Furthermore, you can sit with teachers at the dedicated Doubt Counters to cover any specific topic you missed.
